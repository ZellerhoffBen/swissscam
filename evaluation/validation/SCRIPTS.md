# Recording scripts

Twelve authored conversations, recorded by two team members. The unchanged M4A
files are in `audio/`, named by case ID. `manifest.json` preserves original
filenames and file hashes. These are acted scenarios, not real victim calls.
Keep recordings and scripts fixed when comparing model versions.

## s01

**Caller:** Hi Gran, it's Daniel. I'm using a borrowed phone because mine broke this morning. Can you hear me properly?

**Receiver:** Yes, I can hear you. What happened to your phone?

**Caller:** I dropped it while I was out. I'm at a garage now and there's a problem with the bill.

**Receiver:** Are you all right? Should I call your mum?

**Caller:** Don't tell Mum. I need you to withdraw two thousand francs now and give the cash to a friend I'll send to your house. Please do it before anyone finds out.

**Receiver:** Wait a moment. I need to understand what you're asking.

## s05

**Caller:** Good afternoon. This is Mr Adams. I'm calling about an incident involving your daughter this morning.

**Receiver:** An incident? Is she hurt?

**Caller:** She has been arrested after a traffic accident. To release her today, you must bring five thousand francs in cash to our courier outside the station. Do not contact anyone else.

**Receiver:** Can I speak to her first?

**Caller:** There is no time for that. The courier is waiting, and she will stay in custody if you delay.

**Receiver:** I haven't agreed to anything. Please explain who you work for.

## s09

**Caller:** Good evening. This is Inspector Lewis. We are contacting residents in your area about a recent series of burglaries.

**Receiver:** I see. Has something happened on my street?

**Caller:** We believe some houses nearby may be targeted. Do you keep any jewellery or cash at home?

**Receiver:** I have a few things. Why do you ask?

**Caller:** Put your jewellery and cash into a bag. An undercover officer will collect it tonight for safekeeping. Don't tell your neighbours or call the local station because that could expose the operation.

**Receiver:** I'd like to verify this before anyone comes to my door.

## s13

**Caller:** Hello, this is Rachel from the account security team at City Bank. We are checking a recent card purchase.

**Receiver:** Which purchase are you calling about?

**Caller:** A payment of three hundred and twenty francs at an electronics shop. Did you make that purchase today?

**Receiver:** No, I haven't used my card today.

**Caller:** To cancel it, read me the six-digit login code that has just arrived on your phone. I need that code before I can block the payment.

**Receiver:** The message says not to share it. Why do you need it?

## s17

**Caller:** Hello, I'm calling from the computer service team. Are you near your laptop at the moment?

**Receiver:** Yes, but I haven't requested any support.

**Caller:** We've detected a virus on it. Install our remote control program, give me the access code, and open your online banking so I can check whether the virus has reached your account.

**Receiver:** Why would you need to see my banking?

**Caller:** The security check won't work unless I can control the computer while you sign in.

**Receiver:** I'm going to stop and check this first.

## s20

**Caller:** Good afternoon. This is the customer refund department. We have a refund connected to your computer service contract.

**Receiver:** How much is the refund supposed to be?

**Caller:** Two hundred francs. To release it, tell me your online banking password and the verification code on your phone. I will log in and put the refund straight into your account.

**Receiver:** Can't you refund the card I originally used?

**Caller:** Our refund system needs those login details. Please read them to me before this session expires.

**Receiver:** I'm not going to give you access to my account.

## n01

**Caller:** Hello, this is Mia from the dental practice. I'm calling about your check-up next Tuesday at ten in the morning.

**Receiver:** Hello. Yes, I have that in my calendar.

**Caller:** The dentist has a meeting that morning. Would you be able to come at eleven instead? The appointment will still take about half an hour.

**Receiver:** Eleven works for me. Do I need to bring anything?

**Caller:** Just your usual insurance card when you come to reception. We don't need any details over the phone today.

**Receiver:** Great, thanks for letting me know. I'll see you on Tuesday.

## n03

**Caller:** Hi Dad, it's Emma. The train has been cancelled and I'm waiting at the station. Could you pick me up when you finish work?

**Receiver:** Of course. Which entrance are you waiting at?

**Caller:** The one beside the bakery. It's raining quite hard, so I'm standing inside for now. There's no rush; I have a book with me.

**Receiver:** I can be there in about twenty minutes. Would you like me to bring your coat?

**Caller:** Yes, please. It's on the chair by the kitchen door. I'll watch for your car.

**Receiver:** All right. Stay inside and I'll call when I arrive.

## n06

**Caller:** Hello, this is the town library. The book you reserved last week has arrived, and I'm calling to let you know it's ready.

**Receiver:** Lovely. How long can you hold it for me?

**Caller:** We'll keep it at the front desk until Saturday. Bring your library card when you come in and we'll check it out for you.

**Receiver:** I can visit tomorrow afternoon. Are you open after four?

**Caller:** Yes, we close at seven on Thursdays. The entrance on the square is open as usual.

**Receiver:** Thank you. I'll pick it up tomorrow.

## h01

**Caller:** Hello, this is Claire from City Bank. We noticed an unusual card payment and have temporarily frozen the card as a precaution.

**Receiver:** I haven't bought anything today. What should I do?

**Caller:** Please open your usual banking app and review the payment there, or call the number printed on your card. You can also visit your branch.

**Receiver:** Do you need my password or a verification code?

**Caller:** No. Keep both private. I'm only notifying you; you don't need to give me any account details or move any money on this call.

**Receiver:** Thank you. I'll contact the bank using the number on my card.

## h03

**Caller:** Hi Mum, it's Laura. You mentioned getting a strange bank call yesterday, and I wanted to check you're all right.

**Receiver:** I'm fine, but the caller sounded very convincing. They said something about a security code.

**Caller:** Please don't share a banking password or a one-time code with anyone who calls you. If you're worried, hang up and contact your bank using your card or the official app.

**Receiver:** I ended the call without telling them anything.

**Caller:** Good. We can look through your account together when I visit, but keep your login details to yourself.

**Receiver:** Thank you. I'll see you tomorrow.

## h07

**Caller:** Hello, this is Sam from the internet helpdesk, returning the support request you opened about your connection dropping.

**Receiver:** Thanks for calling back. It has stopped working twice this afternoon.

**Caller:** Let's try restarting the router. Unplug its power cable, wait twenty seconds, and plug it back in. I'll stay on the line while the lights come back.

**Receiver:** Do you need the Wi-Fi password from the label?

**Caller:** No, keep that private. Just tell me whether the internet light turns green again.

**Receiver:** It's green now, and the page on my laptop is loading.
